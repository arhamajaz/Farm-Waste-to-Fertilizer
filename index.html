Module 02: Advanced R&D & Product Formulation

Product Formulation & Future Scope

Pioneering organo-mineral bio-inoculation, smart biopolymer nano-delivery systems, and pyrolyzed biochar carriers engineered for extreme shelf life in tropical Indian soils.

Microbiological Engineering

Synergistic Nitrogen-Fixers & Phosphate-Solubilizers

Consortium CFU > 1×10⁸/g

Combining free-living and symbiotic diazotrophs with mineral-chelating phosphate-solubilizers . This co-inoculation generates a persistent rhizosphere biofilm that feeds crops continuously through reproductive stages.

🌱

Biological Nitrogen Fixation

Azotobacter chroococcum and Rhizobium strains biologically fix 35–45 kg of atmospheric N per hectare per season, drastically lowering synthetic urea input requirements.

⚡

P-Solubilization & Chelation

Bacillus megaterium and Pseudomonas putida secrete low-molecular-weight organic acids, unlocking locked-up legacy phosphorus in calcareous Indian soils.

🛡️

Potassium Mobilization & Auxins

Frateuria aurantia mobilizes insoluble silicate potash minerals while synthesizing natural indole-3-acetic acid (IAA) to promote intense root branching.

Smart Nanotechnology

Chitosan Nanocomposites for Controlled Slow-Release

100% Biodegradable

Encapsulating active bio-inoculants and mineral micronutrients inside biodegradable chitosan nanocomposites . Rather than dissolving in the first rainfall, the smart biopolymer matrix exhibits controlled-release kinetics .

🧪

Cross-Linked Nanopores

Sodium tripolyphosphate (TPP) cross-linking creates sub-100nm pores that physically trap bacteria and humic compounds, shielding them against ultraviolet light and thermal decay.

💧

Rhizosphere Exudate Trigger

Enzymes secreted by hungry root tips (malate, succinate) trigger osmotic swelling in the chitosan membrane, releasing nutrients exactly when the plant signals nutrient demand.

🛡️

Inherent Antifungal Action

Protonated polycationic amine groups in chitosan disrupt cell membranes of pathogenic fungi (Fusarium, Rhizoctonia), providing natural systemic disease suppression.

Key Field Agronomic Benefits:
3x Release Window Extended nutrition from 14 to 100+ days
-65% Runoff Loss Zero flush washout in heavy monsoon rains
+28% Higher Yield Enhanced grain filling & test weight
Zero Toxic Residue 100% soil assimilation without microplastics
Circular Solid Carrier

Pyrolyzed Biochar Carriers Replacing Fossil Lignite

18-Month Shelf Life

Displacing conventional Indian fossil-mined lignite carriers with engineered torrefied agricultural recalcitrant biochar . Its interconnected network of microbial refugia dramatically prolongs retail shelf life.

🔬

High Porous Surface Area

Pyrolysis generates >350 m²/g internal surface area with micropores perfectly sized to cradle bacterial cells, preventing moisture loss and desiccation death.

🌡️

Thermal Insulation Refugia

Carbon matrix buffers against intense summer warehouse heat (up to 46°C in Punjab and Rajasthan), maintaining viable counts where lignite bacteria perish.

🔄

100% Circular & Fossil-Free

Converts local farm-waste paddy straw into high-value carrier media, avoiding coal pit mining while permanently sequestering stable carbon in farm topsoil.
